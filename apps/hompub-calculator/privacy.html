Privacy Policy

Effective September 2026 Applies to HomPub Calculator, v1.0 com.hompub.calculator
The short version

HomPub Calculator does not collect, store, transmit, or share any of your data. It does not request a single Android permission, has no network access, shows no ads, and includes no analytics or tracking software of any kind.

Everything below is the detail behind that one sentence.

Information we collect

None. HomPub Calculator has no account system, no sign-in, and no server — there is nothing for it to send your information to, because it doesn't connect to the internet at all. The numbers you type never leave your device.

Calculation history and the home screen widget

Your recent calculations and whatever's on the home screen widget are kept in ordinary local app storage on your own device, using Android's standard on-device preferences store. This data:

  • never leaves your device
  • is not readable by other apps
  • is deleted automatically if you uninstall the app or clear its storage from Android Settings

Third-party services

There are none. No advertising network, no analytics SDK, no crash reporting service, and no Firebase or similar platform is included in this app. Nothing about your use of this app is visible to anyone but you.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data from anyone, it collects no data from children either. HomPub Calculator is safe for use by anyone, of any age, on the same terms described above.

Changes to this policy

If this app ever changes in a way that affects this policy — for instance, if a future version adds a feature that requires a permission — this page will be updated first, and the "Effective" date above will change accordingly.

Contact
