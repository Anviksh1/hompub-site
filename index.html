Small apps that do one thing, and don't watch you do it.

HomPub builds small apps for Android, iOS and the web with no ads, no accounts, no analytics. Every app keeps your data on your device — and the one that needs a server forgets everything the moment class ends. Nothing about how you use them is anyone's business but yours.
